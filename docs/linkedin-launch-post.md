## Draft copy

I’ve been building a small laboratory for Conway’s Game of Life: a place where
curious beginners and builders can watch motion, draw their own patterns, and
turn experiments into shareable remixes.

The rule is wonderfully spare. A dead cell is born with exactly three live
neighbors. A live cell survives with two or three. Every cell updates
simultaneously, so simple local rules can make gliders, oscillators, art, and
surprising logic.

Life Lab now connects a Playground for immediate experiments, Studio for
editing and versioned projects, verified Conway AND/OR/NOT gates, a binary
half-adder, and a Community flow for publishing, favoriting, commenting, and
attributed remixing. The circuits use finite trains of real cells and observed
output probes, which makes the timing part of the lesson.

I’m especially interested in the overlap between computation and visual making:
motion that becomes a composition, logic that becomes a pattern, and a remix
that gives someone else a starting point. This release is deliberately a
learning lab rather than a claim to ship a full CPU or calculator.
